## Intent

A promotional video for Noir Wallet — high engagement and premium. Noir is an
Android Stellar wallet where an NFC card (NTAG213) pays through its own
constrained on-chain agent (max amount / asset / expiry), via an x402 tap-to-pay
flow on Soroban. The video sells the feeling: a black-and-gold, luxury-card
aesthetic; "Tap into trust".

User asked for "do both": AI voiceover **and** bold kinetic on-screen text over a
premium music bed. On-screen copy stays as short punch lines (2–4 words), not a
transcript of the narration.
