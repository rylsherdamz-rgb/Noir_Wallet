NOIR — TAP INTO TRUST
Noir Wallet: open-source Stellar wallet (Android, Testnet). Link an NFC card (NTAG213) to your wallet;
each card pays through its own on-chain agent with a constrained policy (max amount, asset, expiry).
Fund the card's tap balance (escrow). Tap the card on a merchant phone (x402 tap-to-pay) — payment
authorized and settled on Stellar Soroban. Revoke a card anytime: its tap balance and agent XLM sweep
back to your main wallet. Flow: register device -> register agent (policy) -> fund escrow -> tap ->
authorize -> claim. MIT licensed, Stellar Community Fund Instaward.
